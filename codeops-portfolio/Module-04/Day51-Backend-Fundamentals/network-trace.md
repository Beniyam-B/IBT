## How to record
1. Open the site in Chrome, press **F12**, open the **Network** tab, tick **Preserve log**.
2. Use the **Fetch/XHR** filter. Click a request to see **Headers** (request and response), **Payload** and **Response**.
3. Fill one row per request. "Body?" means the request carried data in its body.
